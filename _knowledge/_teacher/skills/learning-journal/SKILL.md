# The learning journal

## Where it lives

The journal goes in the **private location** from provisioning (or the
platform's persistent memory), not in the course folder. It holds answer
keys and candid notes. The **course folder** holds what the learner owns:
lessons, their exercises and solutions, and their own notes.

## What it holds

- **Profile**: background, false friends, preferred explanations (what
  worked: pictures, analogies, code).
- **Goal**: the objectives and the capstone as agreed (`learning-goal-intake`).
- **Curriculum**: modules with status *not started / in progress /
  checkpoint passed / skipped (gap noted)*.
- **Review queue**: one line per item, with the concept, the misconception
  or the reason, the variant to ask, the due date, and the streak. Spacing:
  next session, then about 3 days, then about 1 week, then about 3 weeks. A
  miss resets it to next session. An item leaves the queue after a hit at
  the three-week interval.
- **Optional later**: parked tangents and out-of-goal interests.
- **Session log**: date, length, what was covered, the recap.

## Rules

- Update it after **every** marked attempt, not just at the end. Sessions
  end abruptly.
- Never inflate or tidy it. A skipped module stays marked as skipped
  (`_teacher` rule 11).
- At the start of a session, read it back and tell the learner where they stand
  in two lines (`teaching-loop`).

## Progress report (on request, or to a creator)

1. Goal and objectives, each marked *demonstrated / in progress / not
   started*, with the evidence: the checkpoint or exercise that showed it.
2. Modules done, and the time spent compared with the plan.
3. Open misconceptions and recurring mistakes, quoted from the learner's own
   attempts.
4. Strengths the learner can build on.
5. The next three steps.

When the report goes to a creator, remember that the learner may read it.
Keep it factual and encouraging.
