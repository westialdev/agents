# Suggestion backlog — parked until called

**The human's ideas come first.** When you, or a teammate, think of a run worth doing while the
human is in the middle of their own idea, do not start it and do not derail the conversation.
Park it here. **The human decides what runs and when.** Nothing on this list is scheduled.

## Scope: the live branches of the path

The list holds the **live branches of the path being followed together**: the alternatives,
follow-ups and checks that the work so far has opened and nobody has closed. A branch the
human ruled out leaves the list, struck through with the ruling and its date. It is a result,
so it is kept.

At the foot, in their own section, sit **DECISIONS WAITING ON YOU**: the standing choices only
the human can make (scope, money, priorities, consent). Keep them visually separate from the
work above.

This file is where everything the agent authors goes. The human's own notes live in the
post-it file (skill `parked-ideas`), and nothing from here is copied there.

## Each item

```markdown
- **Rerun the study on the morning session.** Answers: does the filter's edge survive outside
  the evening? Cost: one run, about an hour, no paid compute. Waits on: nothing.
  Suggested by: the analyst, 2026-10-27.
```

- **What it answers.** One question. An item that answers nothing is not a branch.
- **What it costs**: time, money, compute, and the human's attention. Say when it spends
  something that is metered.
- **What it waits on**, if anything.
- **Who suggested it, and when.**

## Lifecycle

- **Done:** strike it through and date it, with a one-line result and where the result lives.
  **Never delete it.** A "no" is a measurement under stated conditions, and it stays reviewable.
- **Newest at the top** within each section.
- **Re-verify before re-listing.** When you present the list, check each open item and each
  decision against the current state. An item assumed closed may still be live, and one
  assumed open may already be done.
- **Archive, do not prune.** When the file grows unwieldy, move done items to a dated archive
  file and link it from the top.

## When presenting it

Give the human the open items with their costs, and the decisions waiting on them. Recommend an
order if asked, but do not start anything until the human calls it.
